# How to Use Attendly 📚

Attendly is your personal college attendance tracker designed to help you stay eligible and succeed. Follow this guide to get the most out of the platform.

---

## 1. Getting Started 🚀
- **Home Page**: Explore the landing page to understand what Attendly offers.
- **Sign Up**: Create a new account using a unique username and password.
- **Login**: Access your existing data securely.

---

## 2. Initial Setup ⚙️
After your first login, you'll go through a quick setup process:

### A. Current Progress (Optional)
- Enter your **current overall attendance percentage** if you want to continue tracking from where you left off.
- You can skip this to start fresh with a full dashboard.

### B. Configure Your Schedule
1. **College Timings**: Set your daily start and end times, period duration, and lunch break details.
2. **Subjects**: Add all the subjects you are currently studying this semester.
3. **Timetable**: Map your subjects to their respective time slots for each day of the week.

---

## 3. Daily Usage 📅
- **Dashboard**: Your central hub for daily tracking.
- **Marking Attendance**: 
    - At the end of each class or day, mark yourself as **Present** ✅ or **Absent** ❌ for each slot.
    - The system automatically updates your stats in real-time.
- **Simple vs. Full Dashboard**:
    - **Simple**: A streamlined view focused on your daily tasks.
    - **Full**: Comprehensive analytics and subject-wise breakdown.

---

## 4. Understanding Your Stats 📊
- **Overall Attendance**: Your total percentage across all subjects.
- **Subject-Wise Stats**: Track individual subject performance.
- **Eligibility Alerts**:
    - Attendly calculates how many classes you can afford to miss or how many you MUST attend to reach your target (e.g., 75%).
    - **Green**: You're on track!
    - **Red**: Caution! You need to attend more classes.

---

## 5. Tips for Success 💡
- **Be Consistent**: Mark your attendance daily for the most accurate results.
- **Update Timetable**: If your schedule changes, visit the setup page to adjust your timetable.
- **Monitor Trends**: Use the subject-wise stats to identify which areas need more focus.

---

*Set goals, mark daily, and succeed with Attendly!*
